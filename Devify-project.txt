# Smart Test System
### AI-Powered Online Examination Platform
> **"Conduct 100% fair, cheat-proof exams online — no human proctors needed, no extra software, zero compromise."**

---

## 🔍 The Problem We Solve
Every day, thousands of students cheat on online exams. Institutions either spend heavily on manual human supervisors or accept the compromise of academic integrity. We built the **Smart Test System** to eliminate both challenges—delivering total exam integrity automatically, at a fraction of the cost.

---

## ⚡ Core Functions
*   **Real-Time AI Proctoring:** Watches the student through their webcam and microphone to detect face absence, additional people, unauthorized devices (phones, books), and speaking/noise.
*   **Secure Exam Creation:** Permits teachers to build exams with department locking, unique access keys, question banks, and custom durations.
*   **Tamper-Proof Student Environment:** Forces full-screen mode, disables copy/paste and right-clicks, and auto-submits on time-outs.
*   **Audit-Ready Violation Logs:** Logs every single infraction with exact timestamps for post-exam review and dispute resolution.
*   **Instant Notifications:** Alerting examiners in real-time when exams start, finish, or face critical proctoring flags.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
We developed this platform using robust, industry-grade technologies to guarantee performance directly inside the browser:

| Layer | Technology Used |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Backend & APIs** | PHP 8 (High performance, secure, and lightweight) |
| **Database Layer** | MySQL (Structured, normalized, and indexed for speed) |
| **User Interface** | HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap 5 (Responsive, fast load times) |
| **Computer Vision AI** | MediaPipe, face-api.js, TensorFlow.js (On-device face/object tracking) |
| **Audio Intelligence** | Web Audio API, Web Speech API (Real-time volume & keyword analysis) |
| **Security & Anti-Tamper** | WebGL API, Canvas API, Page Visibility API (VM and tab-switch detection) |

*Everything runs directly in the student's browser. No installations, no browser extensions, and no third-party subscription fees required.*

## 🌍 Real-World & Day-to-Day Impact
This solution provides deep positive change across education and enterprise sectors:

*   **For Institutions & Teachers:** Saves thousands of hours and substantial operational budgets by automating invigilation. Educators can deploy hundreds of exams concurrently and review automatically flagged violations.
*   **For Students:** Ensures a level playing field. Honest, hard-working students are protected from being disadvantaged by peer cheating.
*   **For Society & Accessibility:** Enables students in remote or underserved areas to take secure, verified certification exams from home, saving travel costs and expanding educational equity.
*   **For the Environment:** Promotes a 100% digital, paperless examination workflow.
