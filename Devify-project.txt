───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Every day, thousands of students cheat on online exams. Institutions either
  spend heavily on human supervisors or accept the risk. We built Smart Test
  System to eliminate both problems — delivering exam integrity automatically,
  at a fraction of the cost.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  WHAT IT DOES  (Core Functions)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ◉  REAL-TIME AI PROCTORING
     The system watches the student through their own webcam and microphone
     during the entire exam. It detects:

       • Face turned away or absent from screen
       • A second person appearing in the frame
       • Mobile phone, book, or laptop visible on camera
       • Student speaking or unusual background noise
       • Suspicious keywords spoken (e.g. "Hey Google", "Alexa")

     On any violation, the exam AUTOMATICALLY freezes and the examiner
     is notified instantly — no human supervisor required.

  ◉  SECURE EXAM CREATION (Examiner Dashboard)
     Teachers and institutions can:

       • Create exams in minutes with custom duration, marks & subjects
       • Lock exams to specific college departments or branches
       • Protect access with a unique Secret Key per exam
       • Upload questions with images, edit, and manage a question bank
       • View every student's score, violation history & result report

  ◉  STUDENT EXAM ENVIRONMENT
     Students get a distraction-free, tamper-proof interface:

       • Full-screen mode — cannot exit without triggering a warning
       • Copy, paste & right-click are fully disabled
       • Automatic timer with instant submission on timeout
       • Real-time on-screen warnings for any detected rule break

  ◉  COMPLETE VIOLATION AUDIT LOG
     Every single suspicious event is recorded with a timestamp.
     Examiners get a forensic-level report after each exam — perfect for
     appeals, grievance handling, and institutional compliance.

  ◉  SMART NOTIFICATIONS
     Examiners receive live alerts the moment a student starts or
     submits an exam, and are notified instantly of any critical violation.
